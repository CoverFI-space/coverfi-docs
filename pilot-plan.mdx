# External testnet pilot kit

Prepared 9 November 2026. Status: ready-to-use plan, not a completed external pilot. Founder reports current app use is limited to the team. Independently verified external testers: zero. The two published smoke positions are team-controlled automated tests, not independent user validation.

## Outcome target after app integration

Target ten independent testers completing twenty positions in aggregate during the funded pilot. These are proposed future outcomes, not universal SCF admission requirements. Do not pay acquisition incentives or put real funds at risk. If fewer participate, publish the actual count and feedback rather than inventing results.

## Operator preparation

1. The founder connects the wallet app/backend to the new four-contract ABI and published testnet IDs.
2. Display mock-USDC issuer, CFUSD payout units, testnet network, simulated prices, expiry rule, fee breakdown and payout cap before signing.
3. Confirm the two-publisher test feed and monitoring; do not rely on a stale observation or an unconfigured continuous worker.
4. Run one internal UI walkthrough to confirm quote, purchase, expiry settlement, claim and principal withdrawal, then invite independent participants.

## Participant tasks

- Use a separate testnet wallet controlled by the tester. Never share its seed or private key with the team.
- Explain back the premium, cap, expiry condition and payout token; record misunderstandings.
- Open and settle one no-loss position and one controlled simulated-depeg position. Claim an eligible payout and withdraw principal.
- Record wallet-signed transaction hashes, completion time, failed steps and short feedback. Stale/missing-price drills may be operator-led; do not strand testers' real assets.
- Test at least one second browser/mobile wallet configuration where practical. Do not count the same human twice for using multiple wallets.

Exclude team members, assistant-created wallets, bots and team-controlled scripts from the independent-user count. The current smoke buyer is excluded. Obtain consent before publishing a tester's public wallet or feedback. Keep contact details and consent records private; publish a pseudonymous evidence summary.

## Evidence template

Use pilot/tester-evidence-template.csv for consented pseudonymous results. An empty template is not traction evidence. Count actual independent humans and completed positions from the submitted rows; label simulations and report blockers, abandonments and unresolved claims.
