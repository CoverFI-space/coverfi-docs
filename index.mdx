CoverFi is narrowing its funding proposal to one USDC testnet protection market on Stellar/Soroban. The funded primitive is the protection engine and reserve vault, with only necessary custody and price-feed support.

This is a proposed 12-week, USD 50,000 pilot. Mainnet is not recorded as deployed. The fresh four-contract deployment is hash-matched and smoke-verified at testnet ledger 5627678 using team-controlled mock assets; older IDs remain archived as unverified. No verified external LP commitment, paid volume or live partner integration is claimed.
