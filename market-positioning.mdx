# Market and product positioning

Reviewed 9 November 2026. This is a narrow product comparison and a validation plan, not a claim of proven product-market fit, better security, lower prices or market exclusivity. No market-size or revenue forecast has been invented.

## Intended early customer

A Stellar wallet user or application that wants a short-duration, disclosed, capped price-loss position without implementing its own reserve accounting. Validate whether that user accepts the premium, principal lock, oracle dependence and capped recovery. Current verified activity is team-controlled; independent willingness to pay is unproven.

## Alternatives and design differences

| Alternative | Documented approach / CoverFi distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Nexus Mutual cover | Its current docs describe membership and an evidence-based claim assessment process. CoverFi's focused source computes a pre-specified capped expiry-price payout on Soroban and locks maximum capacity at position creation. This shifts trust toward oracle/contract operation; it is not a claim of broader coverage or better protection. |
| InsurAce stablecoin depeg cover | Its published wording describes claims submission, investigation and assessment/voting. CoverFi tests a deterministic entry-to-expiry price calculation instead. The compared documentation does not establish current product availability, liquidity or prices. |
| Selling, diversifying or minimizing stablecoin holding time | Avoiding exposure can be simpler than paying a premium and locking principal. The pilot must show when users prefer CoverFi despite these costs and limitations. |

Sources checked: [Nexus claim assessment](https://docs.nexusmutual.io/protocol/claims-assessment/), [Nexus membership](https://docs.nexusmutual.io/using/become-a-member/), [InsurAce depeg documentation](https://docs.insurace.io/landing-page/documentation/cover-products/stablecoin-de-peg-cover).

## What the pilot must establish

Record the user's actual problem, holding period, accepted payout cap, understandable quote, willingness to pay and reasons for abandoning a position. Seek confirmation from the prospective partner that the core protection flow solves an integration need. A Tael repost or a build-station award supports ecosystem acknowledgement, but does not answer these purchase/LP questions on its own.

Success is a comprehensible Stellar-native flow with reconciled payouts and demonstrable demand. Missing evidence should remain missing, not be replaced with claims that CoverFi is the first or only such product.
